Why this date works

It is a 1:30 PM Saturday start — confirmed against the NHL's official schedule (StubHub and eTickets list 3:00 PM; they are wrong). Watch-outs

Your jerseys will get you turned away. Chelsea and Meatpacking doors enforce upscale-casual: no athletic wear, no sports jerseys, collared shirt and clean shoes. Eighteen guys arriving in Devils gear at midnight is the most likely way this night goes sideways. Either book a table — a reservation generally trumps the dress code — or build a change into the schedule.
February rules out the outdoor plan. Rooftops and harbor cruises are closed or running heated igloos from January through March. Keep the night indoors.
  1. Clubs are dead before midnight. Manhattan rooms crest 12:00–1:30 AM; a 10 PM arrival buys you an empty room and an awkward forty minutes. Brooklyn warehouses peak 1–3 AM.
  2. Book 3–6 weeks out for both the suite and any private dining room, and get the F&B minimum, gratuity policy and cancellation window in writing before you put down a deposit.
  3. Bags: Prudential Center bans all backpacks, including drawstring. Totes under 12×16×6 are fine; paid lockers at Ford Tower and Lafayette Lobby.
  4. Cabs back run $25–50 Manhattan–Brooklyn, more during the 2–4 AM surge. If anyone is heading back to Jersey, the last PATH and NJ Transit trains matter — check the night-of schedule.
